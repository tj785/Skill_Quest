# Character Quest — Student Guide

**Learn → Earn → Build → Achieve → Level up!**

## Logging in

1. Go to the Character Quest website your teacher gives you.
2. **Have a username?** Type your username and password and click **Log in**.
3. **Have a class code instead?** Click **Join a class**, type the code, your first name, pick a username and a password, then **Join and play**. Remember your username and password!

Your progress is saved on the server, so you can log out and come back any time on any computer.

## Controls

Click the world to take control. Press **Esc** to get your mouse back.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** | Walk |
| **Space** | Jump (swim up in water) |
| **Shift** | Run |
| **Mouse** or **arrow keys** | Look around |
| **Right click** or **F** | Place the selected block |
| **Left click** or **R** | Remove a block |

| **V** | Switch between first person and third person |

## Earning blocks: the Question Center

The Quest Center is the big hall with the blue sign in the middle of the village. Walk up and press **E**, or press **Q** anywhere.

- Pick a question. The card shows what you'll earn: XP, skill points, a block and coins.
- Harder questions (Level 3–5) give rarer blocks. A **Master** question gives a Legendary block!
- Got it wrong? No problem. Nothing is taken away. Read it again and try again.
- Questions with a ★ are recommended practice for you.
- **Assignments** from your teacher give a big bonus when you finish all their questions.

## Building

1. Your blocks are in the **hotbar** at the bottom. The number shows how many you have.
2. Look at a spot and **right click** (or press **F**) to place a block.
3. **Left click** (or **R**) removes a block. Blocks you placed go back into your inventory.
4. Your **plot** has a yellow outline and your name. Your teacher decides where you may build: in your plot, in class project zones (blue dashed lines on the map), or anywhere.
5. You can't break the village buildings or what classmates built.
6. Open the **Inventory** (**I**) and click a block to put it in the selected hotbar slot.

Block rarity: **Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary**.

## Leveling up

- XP comes from questions, assignments, achievements and class projects.
- Levels: 1 (0 XP), 2 (100), 3 (250), 4 (500), 5 (800), and higher from there.
- New levels unlock regions: **Forest** (level 1), **Desert** (level 2), **Ocean** (level 3), **Mountains** (level 4).
- Your title grows too: Apprentice, Explorer, Builder, Scholar, Architect, Sage, Legend.
- Check your **Character** screen for your level, skills, achievements and buildings.

## Quests, daily challenges and class events
- Press **L** (or **Quests** in the menu) to see your quests. Each quest has steps you do in order; your current step shows on the right side of the screen.
- **Daily challenges** start fresh every day, and **weekly challenges** every week.
- **Class events** are goals the whole class works on together. When the class reaches the goal, everyone who helped gets a reward, and sometimes something special appears in the village.

## Matching questions
Choose the matching answer for each item from its list. Each answer is used once. Get every pair right to earn the reward. If one is wrong, just try again.

## The shop
Press **B** to open the shop. Spend coins on shirts, hats, capes and titles for your character. Everyone in the world can see what you wear. Some items need a higher level, and some can only be earned from quests and events. Buying things never changes your XP or level.

## Chat
If your teacher turns chat on, press **T** (or tap **Chat**). You may only have a list of friendly phrases to pick from. Your teacher can see every message, so be kind. You can send one message every few seconds.

## When your teacher approves builds
If your teacher chose approval building, your new blocks glow gold and only you and your teacher can see them. When you're happy with your build, press **Submit build for approval** and add a note. When it's approved, everyone can see it. If your teacher sends it back, your blocks go back into your inventory so you can try again.

## Playing on a tablet or phone
Touch controls turn on by themselves. Drag the circle on the left to walk. Drag anywhere else to look around. Use the buttons on the right to **Place**, **Remove**, **Jump**, switch **View** and **Chat**. Tap **Menu** for everything else. You can turn touch controls on or off in **Settings**.

## Being a good builder

- Build in the places your teacher allows.
- Don't try to wreck other people's work; teamwork makes the best worlds.
- Only blocks that stay in the world count for quests, events and project bonuses. Taking a block down and putting it back doesn't count twice.
- If something looks wrong, tell your teacher.

Have fun, and keep learning!
